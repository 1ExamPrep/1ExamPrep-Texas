## How Much Does It Cost to Apply for a Texas Contractor License After Passing the Exam?

Applying for your Texas contractor license after passing the exam typically costs between $100 and $175, depending on the license type and city requirements. These fees include your application submission and the license issuance, but additional costs such as insurance or business setup can add up.

## Factors That Influence How Much You Pay to Apply for Your Texas Contractor License

The final cost you’ll face depends on several elements. Your particular contractor trade mostly determines the licensing body and its fees. For example, electrician licenses go through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) with fees around $115 for the application and license, while plumbing licenses go through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE).

Apart from the base fee, applying for city-specific licenses can hike the price because many large Texas cities like Houston or Dallas administer their own contractor exams and licensing processes with separate fees.

If you want endorsements or multiple categories on your license, such as HVAC and refrigeration or residential plumbing and commercial licensing, you may need to pay additional fees for each.

## Hidden and Additional Costs to Watch For in Your Texas Contractor License Application

Beyond just application fees, there are other expenses that may surprise new licensees:

- **Experience Verification Forms:** You must submit these as part of your application, signed by your supervisors to prove your work experience. Gathering these may take time or administrative costs if you need to request certified copies.
- **Insurance Requirements:** Many contractor licenses require proof of liability and workers’ compensation insurance after your license is approved, which adds ongoing costs.
- **Business Entity Setup:** You might want to register an LLC or corporation to hold the license legally, which means state filing fees and possibly fees for registered agents or legal assistance.
- **Reapplication or Appeals:** If your initial application is incomplete or denied, additional fees for resubmission or appeal processes could apply.

Knowing these ahead of time helps avoid sticker shock and delays once you pass your exam.

## How to Determine Your Fee Level and Avoid Overpaying

Here’s how to find out where your application stands in terms of cost:

- Check the licensing agency’s official website (TDLR or TSBPE) for the exact fee for your license category.
- Contact your city’s licensing board if you’ll need a local contractor license as well.
- Verify if you qualify for any exceptions or fee waivers due to military service, education, or special certifications.
- Skip third-party services that bundle filings unless they provide clear value beyond the standard application process.

Doing your own homework before you pay can save you money and headaches.

## Common Questions About Texas Contractor License Application Costs After Passing the Exam

**What is the standard Texas contractor license application fee?** The usual fee ranges from $100 to $115 for a typical one-year license through state agencies like TDLR or TSBPE.

**Are city licensing fees separate from state fees?** Yes, many Texas cities require their own contractor licenses with separate application fees that vary widely.

**Can I apply online for my Texas contractor license after passing the exam?** Some licenses allow online applications, but availability may fluctuate, so consult the licensing agency’s website for current submission methods.

**Do experience verification forms cost extra?** They usually don’t have fees themselves but obtaining signatures and processed copies might involve minor administrative costs.

**Is insurance included in licensing fees?** No, insurance is a separate requirement you must meet after license approval, which adds to your ongoing business expenses.
