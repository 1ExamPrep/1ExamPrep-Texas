How does the ACR Contractor Class B, Environmental Air exam work?

The ACR Contractor Class B, Environmental Air exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. You apply to TDLR with proof of experience, then schedule the open-book exam with PSI.

  • The PSI exam has 100 items in 170 minutes and costs $60.
  • References include the 2024 IMC, UMC and IFGC, ACCA Manuals J, D and S, and Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, 22nd edition.
  • TDLR licenses air conditioning and refrigeration contractors in two classes: Class B up to 25 tons cooling and 1.5 million BTU per hour heating, and Class A with no limit.
  • A contractor needs 48 months of practical experience in the last 72 months, and the application fee is $115.
  • PSI gives the exams, and they are open book; since September 1, 2026 they reference the 2024 IMC, UMC and IFGC.
  • Contractors complete 8 hours of continuing education each year, including 1 hour of Texas law and rules.

Who issues the ACR Contractor Class B, Environmental Air license or certification in Texas?

It comes from the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

What is the difference between Class A and Class B?

Class B covers cooling systems of 25 tons and under and heating systems of 1.5 million BTU per hour and under. Class A has no size limit and a longer exam: 120 items in 230 minutes for $74, against 100 items in 170 minutes for $60.
