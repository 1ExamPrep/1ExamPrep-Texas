### Open Book Test

The Texas Master Electrician examination is an **open book test**. The current examination reference is the **National Electrical Code, 2026 Edition**.

Candidates are responsible for bringing their own NEC book to the examination center. Texas permits a soft-bound edition of the NEC. Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, and ring-bound NEC copies are not permitted, and the NEC Handbook is not an approved examination-room substitute for the codebook.

The NEC may be highlighted and underlined before the examination session and may contain notes prepared before testing. Current examination rules also permit permanent NEC index tabs manufactured or provided for use with the NEC book. ### State Requirements

Texas Master Electrician applicants must have held a **Journeyman Electrician license for at least two years** and must document at least **12,000 hours of on-the-job electrical training** under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.

The Texas Master Electrician credential authorizes electrical work involving the installation, maintenance, or extension of electrical wiring systems and associated apparatus and equipment within the scope established by state law.

The Master Electrician license should not be confused with the Texas Electrical Contractor license. The Master Electrician license is an individual credential. The Electrical Contractor license applies to the contracting business.

A Texas Electrical Contractor must employ a licensed Master Electrician as the company's **Master Electrician of Record**. The Master Electrician may also own the business. A Master Electrician is generally assigned to only one Electrical Contractor unless the Master Electrician owns more than 50% of the electrical contracting business.

Texas adopted the **2026 National Electrical Code** effective September 1, 2026, with a limited state exception relating to certain GFCI requirements for outdoor outlets serving HVAC equipment.

### Test Information and Study Materials

**Services and Service Equipment:** Services account for significant portions of both examination sections. Review service conductors, service equipment, disconnecting means, grounding, bonding, ratings, overcurrent protection, and service calculations.

**Separately Derived Systems:** Study transformers, system bonding jumpers, grounding electrode conductors, equipment grounding, and Article 250 requirements for separately derived systems.

**Branch Circuits:** Review Article 210 requirements, conductor sizing, required circuits, continuous loads, overcurrent protection, GFCI and AFCI protection, receptacles, and utilization loads.

**Feeders:** Study Article 215 along with feeder conductor sizing, load calculations, neutral requirements, grounding, and overcurrent protection.

**Article 220 Calculations:** Practice dwelling and commercial load calculations, lighting loads, receptacles, appliances, ranges, dryers, motors, heating and cooling equipment, and applicable demand factors.

**Grounding and Bonding:** Article 250 is essential Master Electrician material. Review grounding electrodes, bonding jumpers, equipment grounding conductors, services, feeders, separately derived systems, and conductor sizing.

**Conductors:** Study Article 310 ampacity tables, insulation ratings, temperature limitations, adjustment factors, correction factors, parallel conductors, and termination requirements.

**Wiring Methods:** Review Chapter 3 raceways, cables, boxes, conduit bodies, supports, underground wiring, wet locations, physical protection, raceway fill, and box fill.

**Motors and Generators:** Motor and generator questions form an important part of both portions. Review motor full-load current tables, conductor sizing, overload protection, short-circuit and ground-fault protection, controllers, disconnects, and generator requirements.

**Special Occupancies and Equipment:** Review hazardous locations, health-care facilities, pools, signs, elevators, generators, electric vehicle systems, photovoltaic installations, energy storage, emergency systems, and other specialized NEC applications.

**Renewable Energy Technologies:** Current examination outlines include renewable-energy topics. Review photovoltaic systems, interconnected power sources, energy storage, and related code requirements.

**Timed Navigation:** Practice locating highlighted and tabbed NEC subjects under realistic time limits. The goal is to recognize the subject, move to the correct article, confirm the rule, and return to the question efficiently.

**Calculation Practice:** The separate Calculations Portion deserves dedicated preparation. Work through service, feeder, branch-circuit, conductor, motor, transformer, grounding, raceway, and equipment calculations without relying only on memorized shortcuts.
