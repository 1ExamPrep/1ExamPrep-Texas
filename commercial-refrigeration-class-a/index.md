## How does the ACR Contractor Class A, Commercial Refrigeration exam work?

The ACR Contractor Class A, Commercial Refrigeration exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. You apply to TDLR with proof of experience, then schedule the open-book exam with PSI.

- The PSI exam has 120 items in 230 minutes and costs $74.
- Class A insurance minimums are $300,000 per occurrence and $600,000 aggregate.
- TDLR licenses air conditioning and refrigeration contractors in two classes: Class B up to 25 tons cooling and 1.5 million BTU per hour heating, and Class A with no limit.
- A contractor needs 48 months of practical experience in the last 72 months, and the application fee is $115.
- PSI gives the exams, and they are open book; since September 1, 2026 they reference the 2024 IMC, UMC and IFGC.
- Contractors complete 8 hours of continuing education each year, including 1 hour of Texas law and rules.

Class B covers cooling systems of 25 tons and under and heating systems of 1.5 million BTU per hour and under. Class A has no size limit and a longer exam: 120 items in 230 minutes for $74, against 100 items in 170 minutes for $60.
