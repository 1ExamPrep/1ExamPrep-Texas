### Open Book Test

The Texas Journeyman Electrician examination is an **open book test**.

The approved technical reference is the **National Electrical Code, NEC, 2026 Edition**. Candidates are responsible for bringing an acceptable NEC book to the examination site.

Soft-bound editions of the NEC may be used. Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, and ring-bound copies are not permitted. The NEC Handbook is also not permitted as the examination-room reference.

The NEC may contain permitted highlighting, underlining, notes prepared before the examination, and compliant permanent NEC index tabs. Candidates may not add new notes, highlighting, underlining, indexing, or other markings during the testing session.

The included rental copy of the 2026 NEC is highlighted and tabbed to support preparation. Candidates should use the book during the full **6 months of course access** so that major sections become familiar before exam day.

Open-book testing still requires efficient code use. The more familiar candidates are with the organization of the NEC, the faster they can identify where an answer is likely to be found. Repeated practice with the rental book can help candidates build this familiarity over time.

### Licensing Steps

1. **Gain qualifying electrical experience.** Texas requires supervised on-the-job electrical training before an applicant can obtain the Journeyman Electrician license.
2. **Work under a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.** Qualifying experience must be completed under the required supervision.
3. **Accumulate at least 7,000 qualifying hours before applying to test.** Texas allows an applicant on the standard experience pathway to become eligible for the examination before completing the full licensing-hour requirement.
4. **Complete the full 8,000-hour requirement.** The full experience requirement must be satisfied before the Journeyman Electrician license can be issued.

### State Requirements

The standard Texas Journeyman Electrician pathway requires a total of **8,000 hours of qualifying on-the-job training** under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.

An applicant may submit at least **7,000 hours** to become eligible to take the examination before completing the final 1,000 hours required for licensing.

Qualifying electrical experience must be documented by the Master Electrician or Master Electricians who supervised the work. The experience documentation is submitted as part of the TDLR application process.

Texas also recognizes approved education pathways that may affect how candidates qualify for the Journeyman Electrician examination. Applicants using an approved educational pathway must meet the requirements applicable to that program.

A Texas Journeyman Electrician performs electrical work under the **general supervision of a Master Electrician**.

The Journeyman Electrician license is an individual credential. It is different from a Texas Electrical Contractor business license. A business performing electrical contracting work must meet separate contractor-license requirements.

Texas electrician licenses are subject to annual renewal requirements and applicable continuing education requirements.

### Test Information and Study Materials

**Branch Circuits and Conductors:** This is one of the largest NEC Knowledge categories with 10 items. Review branch-circuit ratings, conductor sizing, GFCI and AFCI protection, continuous loads, grounding, overcurrent protection, and required circuit provisions.

**Electrical Wiring Methods and Materials:** Ten NEC Knowledge items cover raceways, cables, boxes, enclosures, cable trays, underground wiring, conductor protection, fittings, and other Chapter 3 subjects.

**Electrical Equipment and Devices:** Ten items cover receptacles, switches, luminaires, appliances, HVAC equipment, electrical equipment, and workspace clearance.

**Electrical Services:** Review service conductors, service equipment, grounding and bonding, disconnecting means, overcurrent protection, separately derived systems, and service calculations.

**Feeders:** Study feeder conductor sizing, neutral requirements, grounding and bonding, load calculations, equipment grounding conductors, and overcurrent protection.

**Grounding and Bonding:** Article 250 is important across multiple examination categories. Candidates should understand grounding electrode conductors, equipment grounding conductors, bonding jumpers, service bonding, feeder grounding, and separately derived systems.

**Conductor Ampacity:** Practice using Article 310 tables and applying temperature limitations, correction factors, adjustment factors, insulation ratings, and terminal temperature requirements.

**Motors and Generators:** Review motor conductors, overload protection, branch-circuit short-circuit and ground-fault protection, controllers, disconnecting means, generators, and applicable NEC tables.

**Control Devices and Disconnecting Means:** Study switches, controllers, contactors, disconnect ratings, accessibility, grouping, location, and safe equipment isolation.

**Special Occupancies, Equipment, and Conditions:** Review specialized installations such as pools, marinas, mobile homes, emergency systems, special equipment, and other subjects identified in the examination outline.

**Renewable Energy Technologies:** Study photovoltaic systems and electric vehicle charging equipment along with related conductor, disconnect, protection, grounding, and equipment requirements.

**Service Calculations:** Practice demand factors, general lighting loads, appliances, heating and cooling, motors, and other loads used to determine service size.

**Feeder Calculations:** Review feeder loads, conductor sizing, grounded conductors, and overcurrent protection while applying the correct NEC demand factors.

**Branch-Circuit Calculations:** Practice conductor sizing, continuous loads, equipment loads, appliance loads, motor loads, and other branch-circuit calculations.

**Box and Raceway Calculations:** Review box fill, conductor volume allowances, raceway fill, conductor quantities, and related Chapter 3 calculation requirements.

**Code Navigation:** Use the highlighted and tabbed 2026 NEC during online course study. Practice identifying the subject first, finding the correct article or table, and confirming the applicable rule before answering.

**Timed Practice:** The NEC Knowledge Portion allows 130 minutes for 59 total items, while the Calculations Portion allows 110 minutes for 26 total items. Practice each portion under realistic time limits.

**Study by Exam Weight:** Branch Circuits and Conductors, Wiring Methods and Materials, and Electrical Equipment and Devices are the largest NEC Knowledge categories. These subjects deserve regular attention while the smaller categories should still receive complete coverage.

## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

- The PSI exam has two portions: 59 NEC knowledge items in 130 minutes and 26 calculation items in 110 minutes.
- The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $30.
- TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
- Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
- The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
- Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
