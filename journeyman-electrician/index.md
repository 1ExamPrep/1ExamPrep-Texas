## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

- The PSI exam has two portions: 59 NEC knowledge items in 130 minutes and 26 calculation items in 110 minutes.
- The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $30.
- TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
- Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
- The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
- Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.

### Is the Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. Texas electrician exams are open book with your own soft-bound 2026 NEC. Highlighting, underlining and notes made before the exam are fine, but only the publisher's permanent index tabs are allowed; homemade and other tabs are not.

### Who issues the Journeyman Electrician license or certification in Texas?

It comes from the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### What does the Journeyman Electrician exam cost?

PSI charges $78 for the journeyman and master exams, $61 for journeyman lineman and $64 for the other electrician exams, paid to PSI after TDLR approves your application.

### Do I need the 2023 or the 2026 NEC?

The 2026 NEC. Texas adopted it on July 28, 2026, and PSI exams have been written to it since September 1, 2026. Packages built on the 2023 NEC are still listed for anyone finishing up, but the 2026 editions match the current exam.

### What is the Journeyman Electrician exam like?

The PSI exam has two portions: 59 NEC knowledge items in 130 minutes and 26 calculation items in 110 minutes. The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $30.
