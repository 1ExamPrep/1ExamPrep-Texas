### Exam Details

Texas electrician licensing is regulated by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. The Texas Journeyman Electrician examination is administered through PSI after the candidate’s license application has been reviewed and the candidate has been approved to test.

The Texas Journeyman Electrician exam is based on the 2023 National Electrical Code. The examination is open book and allows candidates to reference the 2023 NEC during testing. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved NEC book to the examination site.

The Texas Journeyman Electrician exam contains 80 scored items and has a 240-minute time limit. The exam also includes 5 non-scored items with additional time included for those questions. A score of 70% is required to pass.

Exam content includes definitions, calculations, theory, plans, electrical services, service equipment, separately derived systems, feeders, branch circuit calculations and conductors, wiring methods, electrical materials, electrical equipment and devices, motors, generators, control devices, disconnecting means, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and renewable energy technologies.

Candidates should prepare for both code lookup and calculation-based questions. Texas exam questions may require direct NEC navigation, table use, formula work, understanding of electrical theory, and careful reading of conditions within the question. Unless a question specifically states otherwise, conductors are treated as copper for exam purposes.

### Open Book Test

The Texas Journeyman Electrician exam is an open book test. The approved examination reference is the National Electrical Code, 2023 Edition. Open book testing can be a major advantage for candidates who know how to use the NEC efficiently, but it does not remove the need for serious preparation.

Texas exam rules allow any soft-bound edition of the National Electrical Code for the examination. Loose-leaf, spiralbound, and ring-bound copies of the NEC are not allowed in the examination room. The NEC Handbook is not allowed. Candidates may bring an NEC book that has been highlighted, underlined, or contains notes made before the examination session. Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, index, or mark the NEC book during the exam.

Texas allows NEC books tabbed with permanent index tabs manufactured or provided by the NEC book publisher. Homemade tabs, DIY tabs, and non-NEC tabs are not allowed. Additional paper, whether loose, taped, adhered, stapled, or otherwise attached, is not allowed in the examination room.

The National Electrical Code 2023 Paperback with Tabs supports open book preparation by helping candidates practice code navigation with an exam-ready reference. Candidates should use the code book during regular study sessions, not only on test day. Practice should include locating definitions, branch circuit rules, feeder requirements, service rules, grounding and bonding provisions, conductor ampacity tables, box fill rules, raceway requirements, motor requirements, special occupancy articles, and renewable energy provisions.

### Licensing Steps

The Texas journeyman electrician licensing path begins with supervised electrical work experience. A Journeyman Electrician in Texas is licensed to perform electrical work under the general supervision of a Master Electrician.

To apply for a Texas Journeyman Electrician license, candidates must have completed at least 7,000 hours of on-the-job training under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician. Submitting at least 7,000 hours allows the applicant to take the examination before reaching the full 8,000 hours required for licensure.

A typical licensing path includes registering and working in the electrical trade, completing supervised on-the-job training under a Texas-licensed Master Electrician, gathering experience verification from each supervising Master Electrician, submitting the Journeyman Electrician license application with the required application fee, receiving approval to test, scheduling the examination with PSI, passing the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam, and completing the remaining licensing requirements.

Candidates must obtain verification from each Master Electrician who supervised their work. Experience must be documented and signed by the supervising Master Electrician on the required experience verification form. TDLR may contact individuals to verify submitted experience.

### State Requirements

Texas requires licensure for electrical work performed in the state unless an exemption applies. Electrical work includes labor or material used in installing, maintaining, or extending an electrical wiring system and related apparatus or equipment used with electrical energy in, on, outside, or attached to a building, residence, structure, property, or premises. Service entrance conductors are also included under the electrical work definition.

A Texas Journeyman Electrician license allows the license holder to perform electrical work under the general supervision of a Master Electrician. Candidates preparing for journeyman licensing should understand that examination approval and license issuance are separate steps. TDLR allows applicants with at least 7,000 documented hours to sit for the exam, but 8,000 hours are required for the license.

Experience must be supervised by a Master Electrician licensed in Texas. Candidates should keep accurate employment records, supervisor names, license information, dates of employment, and completed verification forms. Incomplete or missing verification can slow down the application process.

The initial license application must be submitted to TDLR with the required application fee. Electrician licenses are valid for one year from the date of issuance and must be renewed annually. Renewal may be completed online.

Applicants with certain criminal convictions may need to submit additional documentation with the application materials. Candidates should review the application instructions carefully and make sure all required supporting documents are included before submission.

## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

- The PSI exam has two portions: 59 NEC knowledge items in 130 minutes and 26 calculation items in 110 minutes.
- The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $30.
- TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
- Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
- The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
- Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
