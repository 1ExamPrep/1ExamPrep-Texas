### Exam Details

Texas electrician licensing is regulated by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). TDLR provides exam information and administers exams through PSI. The Texas Electrician Candidate Information Bulletin explains that electrician examinations are referenced to the **2023 NEC** and states that the examinations are **open book**, with specific rules for the NEC book you bring into the testing site.

**Texas Journeyman Electrician exam format (as listed in the Candidate Information Bulletin):**

- **Scored questions:** 80 scored items
- **Time allowed:** 240 minutes
- **Passing requirement:** 70% correct to pass
- **Additional items:** 5 non-scored items (additional 20 minutes noted in the bulletin)
- **Examination fee:** $78

**Content outline (Journeyman Electrician):**

- Definitions, Calculations, Theory, and Plans
- Electrical Services, Service Equipment, and Separately Derived Systems
- Electrical Feeders
- Branch Circuit Calculations and Conductors
- Electrical Wiring Methods and Electrical Materials
- Electrical Equipment and Devices
- Motors and Generators
- Electrical Control Devices and Disconnecting Means
- Special Occupancies, Equipment, and Conditions
- Renewable Energy Technologies

TDLR also notes that **starting March 11, 2025**, the Journeyman Electrician exam changed to consist of **two separate parts**: a knowledge portion and a calculations portion, with additional details provided in the candidate bulletin. ### Open Book Test

Texas electrician exams are open book. The Candidate Information Bulletin explains that the exam is referenced to the **2023 NEC**, and candidates are responsible for bringing their own NEC book to the test site. It also provides detailed rules about what types of NEC books and tabs are allowed.

**Key open-book rules you should build into your prep:**

- **Bring a compliant NEC book:** Any **soft-bound** edition of the NEC may be used. Loose-leaf, spiralbound, or ring-bound NEC copies are not allowed in the examination room.
- **Pre-marking rules:** The bulletin states your NEC book may be highlighted, underlined, and/or contain notes *prior* to the examination session.
- **Tab rules matter:** NEC books may be tabbed with **permanent index tabs manufactured and/or provided by the NEC book publisher**. Homemade, DIY, or non-NEC tabs are not allowed.
- **No changes during the exam:** You may not write, highlight, underline, index, or mark your NEC book during the exam session.
- **No extra papers:** Additional paper (loose, taped, adhered, stapled, etc.) is not allowed in the exam room.
- **NEC Handbook not allowed:** The bulletin specifically states the NEC Handbook is not allowed in the examination room.

The tabbed NEC in this combo supports the best open-book strategy: learn the layout, practice targeted lookups, and develop a reliable system for finding answers quickly—without flipping aimlessly or reading entire articles under pressure.

### Licensing Steps

Texas journeyman licensing is managed through TDLR. The state’s application page explains the experience threshold to apply, how the exam approval process works, and how initial licensing is submitted. A typical process looks like this:

1. **Confirm you’re on the Journeyman path.** A Journeyman Electrician in Texas is licensed to perform electrical work under the general supervision of a Master Electrician.
2. **Document your qualifying work experience.** TDLR requires a minimum number of on-the-job training hours under the supervision of a Master Electrician licensed in Texas, with verification from supervisors.
3. **Submit your application to TDLR.** The initial license application must be submitted by mail along with the required application fee.
4. **Receive approval to test.** After TDLR receives and verifies your completed application, you’ll receive notification that you’re approved to take the licensing exam.
5. **Schedule and take the exam through PSI.** TDLR provides exam scheduling guidance through their electrician exam information page.
6. **Complete licensure requirements.** After passing, finish any remaining requirements for the journeyman license level you’re pursuing and keep up with renewal requirements.

### State Requirements

Texas requires electrician licensing for most non-exempt electrical work performed in the state, and licensing is administered by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). TDLR’s Journeyman Electrician application page outlines the experience threshold tied to the journeyman license and explains that applicants must document and obtain verification of hours worked under a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.

**Journeyman experience requirement (TDLR):**

- To apply for a Journeyman Electrician license, you must have completed at least **7,000 hours** of on-the-job training under the supervision of a Master Electrician licensed in Texas.
- TDLR states that submitting at least **7,000 hours** allows you to take the examination before reaching the full **8,000 hours required for licensure**.

**Application and license term (TDLR):**

- **Initial application:** submitted by mail
- **Application fee:** $30 (non-refundable, as stated by TDLR)
- **License term:** electrician licenses are valid for one year from the date of issuance and must be renewed annually (renewals may be completed online)

## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

- The PSI exam has two portions: 59 NEC knowledge items in 130 minutes and 26 calculation items in 110 minutes.
- The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $30.
- TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
- Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
- The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
- Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
