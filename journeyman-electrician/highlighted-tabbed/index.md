### Open Book Test

The Texas Journeyman Electrician examination is an **open book test**.

The approved technical reference for the current examination is the **National Electrical Code, NEC, 2026 Edition**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own acceptable NEC book to the testing center.

Any soft-bound edition of the NEC may be used. Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, and ring-bound NEC copies are not permitted. The NEC Handbook is also not allowed in the examination room.

The NEC may be highlighted, underlined, and contain notes prepared before the examination session. Permanent index tabs manufactured or provided by the NEC publisher are permitted. Homemade, DIY, and non-NEC tabs are not allowed.

Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, index, or otherwise mark the NEC during the examination session. Additional paper that is loose, taped, adhered, stapled, or otherwise inserted into the book is also prohibited.

This highlighted and tabbed package is designed around the value of organized reference navigation. Candidates should still become personally familiar with the location of major subjects, because tabs are most useful when combined with an understanding of the NEC structure.

### Licensing Steps

1. **Gain qualifying electrical experience.** Texas requires supervised on-the-job electrical training for the standard Journeyman Electrician pathway.
2. **Work under a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.** Qualifying experience must be completed under the required supervision.
3. **Accumulate at least 7,000 hours before applying to test.** Texas permits an applicant on the standard pathway to seek examination eligibility after completing at least 7,000 hours of qualifying on-the-job training.
4. **Complete the full 8,000-hour licensing requirement.** Passing the exam does not eliminate the full experience requirement for license issuance.
5. **Obtain experience verification.** Each Master Electrician who supervised qualifying work must verify the applicable experience.
6. **Submit the Journeyman Electrician application.** The application and experience documentation are submitted to TDLR.
7. **Receive examination authorization.** After eligibility is approved, the candidate receives instructions for scheduling through PSI.
8. **Prepare with the 2026 NEC.** Study both the knowledge and calculation content areas using the current code edition.
9. **Take the NEC Knowledge Portion.** Complete 59 items within 130 minutes.
10. **Take the Calculations Portion.** Complete 26 items within 110 minutes.
11. **Pass both examination portions.** A score of at least 70% is required on each portion.
12. **Complete all remaining licensing requirements.** The full experience requirement and applicable TDLR requirements must be satisfied before the license is issued.

### State Requirements

A Texas Journeyman Electrician is licensed to perform electrical work under the **general supervision of a Master Electrician**.

The standard licensing pathway requires **8,000 hours of qualifying on-the-job training** under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician. An applicant may submit at least 7,000 hours to become eligible to take the examination before completing the final 1,000 hours required for licensure.

Qualifying experience must be documented and signed by each supervising Master Electrician. TDLR may verify the experience submitted as part of the license application.

Texas also provides approved education-based pathways that may affect the process for candidates participating in qualifying Journeyman Electrician education programs. Applicants using those pathways must satisfy the requirements specific to the approved program.

The Journeyman Electrician license is an individual credential. It does not independently authorize the holder to operate an electrical contracting business. Texas Electrical Contractors must meet separate business-license requirements and operate through the appropriate Master Electrician of Record structure.

Texas Journeyman Electricians are required to complete four hours of approved continuing education before each license renewal. Required coursework addresses the National Electrical Code, Texas electrician law and administrative rules, and electrical safety.

**Electrical Equipment and Devices:** Ten NEC Knowledge items address receptacles, switches, luminaires, air-conditioning and refrigeration equipment, appliances, and workspace clearance.

**Services:** Review service equipment, fault current, temporary wiring, grounding and bonding, transformers, and the code requirements governing electrical service installations.

**Feeders:** Study feeder load requirements, grounded conductors, grounding and bonding, and feeder overcurrent protection. The calculations portion also includes feeder load calculations.

**Grounding and Bonding:** Article 250 is an essential area for Journeyman preparation. Study equipment grounding conductors, grounding electrode conductors, bonding jumpers, services, feeders, and separately derived systems.

**Calculations:** Practice electrical theory, service loads, feeder calculations, branch-circuit calculations, conductors, raceways, boxes, equipment, motors, and special installations. The calculations portion requires candidates to apply NEC rules numerically rather than simply identify a code requirement.

**Motors and Generators:** Review motor conductor sizing, overload protection, branch-circuit short-circuit and ground-fault protection, controllers, disconnecting means, generators, and applicable tables.

**Special Occupancies, Equipment, and Conditions:** Review special occupancies, swimming pools and fountains, RVs, mobile homes, park trailers, marinas, data processing and communications equipment, emergency systems, and miscellaneous specialized installations.

**Renewable Energy Technologies:** Prepare for photovoltaic systems and electric vehicle charging equipment. These subjects appear in both the knowledge and calculation content outlines.

**Highlighted and Tabbed NEC Navigation:** Use the prepared book during timed practice. Work on recognizing a question category, locating the correct article or table, confirming the requirement, and returning to the question efficiently.

## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

- The PSI exam has two portions: 59 NEC knowledge items in 130 minutes and 26 calculation items in 110 minutes.
- The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $30.
- TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
- Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
- The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
- Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
