### Open Book Test

The Texas Maintenance Electrician examination is an **open book test**.

The technical reference for the current examination is the **National Electrical Code, NEC, 2026 Edition**. Candidates should become comfortable navigating the NEC before examination day so they can locate applicable requirements efficiently.

Acceptable soft-bound NEC editions may be used. Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, and ring-bound copies are not permitted in the testing room. The NEC Handbook is not permitted as the examination reference.

The NEC may contain permitted highlighting, underlining, notes prepared before testing, and compliant permanent NEC index tabs. Candidates may not add new notes, highlighting, underlining, indexing, or other markings during the examination session.

### State Requirements

A Texas Maintenance Electrician performs electrical maintenance work under the **general supervision of a Master Electrician**.

Applicants must document at least **8,000 hours of on-the-job training** under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician or Maintenance Electrician.

Qualifying experience must be properly documented as part of the state licensing process. TDLR reviews the application and supporting experience information before approving eligible applicants to take the examination.

The Maintenance Electrician credential has a more limited scope than the Texas Journeyman Electrician license. It focuses specifically on electrical maintenance work rather than the broader range of electrical work covered by the Journeyman Electrician credential.

Maintenance electrical work may involve existing feeders, branch circuits, panels, lighting, appliances, motors, controls, HVAC equipment, grounding systems, disconnecting means, and installed wiring systems. Candidates should therefore be prepared to apply NEC requirements to a range of maintenance-related situations.

The Maintenance Electrician license is an individual credential. Operating an electrical contracting business can involve separate business licensing and compliance obligations beyond holding the individual Maintenance Electrician license.

### Test Information and Study Materials

**Definitions, Calculations, Theory, and Plans:** Review Article 100 definitions, basic electrical theory, voltage, current, resistance, power, plan interpretation, and common electrical calculations.

**Electrical Services:** Study service conductors, service equipment, disconnects, grounding, bonding, equipment ratings, and overcurrent protection.

**Separately Derived Systems:** Review transformers, grounding electrode conductors, system bonding jumpers, equipment grounding conductors, and applicable Article 250 requirements.

**Electrical Feeders:** Study feeder conductor sizing, loads, grounded conductors, overcurrent protection, grounding, and bonding.

**Branch Circuit Calculations and Conductors:** This is one of the largest examination categories. Review conductor ampacity, circuit ratings, continuous loads, GFCI protection, AFCI protection, conductor sizing, and overcurrent protection.

**Grounding and Bonding:** Article 250 applies across several examination categories. Review grounding electrode systems, equipment grounding conductors, bonding jumpers, service bonding, feeder grounding, and separately derived systems.

**Electrical Wiring Methods and Materials:** Study raceways, cables, conductors, boxes, fittings, enclosures, securing and supporting, physical protection, and environmental requirements.

**Electrical Equipment and Devices:** This category contains 10 scored items. Review receptacles, switches, appliances, luminaires, HVAC equipment, disconnects, equipment ratings, and working-clearance requirements.

**Motors and Generators:** Review motor conductors, overload protection, branch-circuit short-circuit and ground-fault protection, controllers, disconnects, and generator requirements.

**Controls and Disconnecting Means:** Study controllers, contactors, switches, relays, equipment disconnects, accessibility, ratings, and safe equipment isolation.

**Special Occupancies, Equipment, and Conditions:** Eight scored items cover specialized NEC requirements. Candidates should review the code chapters addressing installations that require rules beyond general wiring provisions.

## How does the Maintenance Electrician exam work?

The Maintenance Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

- The PSI exam has 60 scored items in 180 minutes and costs $64.
- The TDLR application fee is $20.
- TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
- Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
- The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
- Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
