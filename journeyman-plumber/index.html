How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. You apply to TSBPE, then take the written exam at Pearson VUE and, where required, the practical in Austin or Waco.

  • The written exam has 100 items in 120 minutes and costs $68.50 at Pearson VUE; the practical has 6 items in 240 minutes.
  • TSBPE charges $40 to apply for the exam and $40 for the license.
  • The Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE) licenses plumbers, not TDLR, and Pearson VUE gives the computer exams.
  • The written exams are closed book: no books, papers or references are allowed in the room, so what you study is what you have.
  • Journeyman and tradesman candidates also take a hands-on practical, held only in Austin and Waco.
  • The current handbook points to the 2018 IPC with the 2018 IFGC, or the 2018 UPC; a move to the 2021 codes was proposed in July 2026.

Is the Journeyman Plumber exam open book?

No. The Texas plumbing written exams are closed book. Pearson VUE does not allow books, papers or reference material in the room, so the study guide and practice exams are how you prepare.

Who issues the Journeyman Plumber license or certification in Texas?

It comes from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Is there a practical exam?

Yes, for journeyman and tradesman candidates. The practical is held only at the TSBPE testing centers in Austin and Waco. A tradesman moving up to journeyman takes the written exam only.

How long do I wait to retake a failed exam?

Pearson VUE lists 30 days after a first failure, 60 days after a second and 90 days after each later one.

What is the Journeyman Plumber exam like?

The written exam has 100 items in 120 minutes and costs $68.50 at Pearson VUE; the practical has 6 items in 240 minutes. TSBPE charges $40 to apply for the exam and $40 for the license.
