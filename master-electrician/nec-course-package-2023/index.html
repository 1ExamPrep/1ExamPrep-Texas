Exam Details

The Texas Master Electrician examination is administered by PSI for the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. Applicants must first submit their Master Electrician license application to TDLR and receive approval before scheduling the examination.

The current Texas Master Electrician examination consists of two separate multiple-choice portions: the NEC Knowledge Portion and the Calculations Portion. Candidates must earn at least 70 percent on each portion.

The NEC Knowledge Portion contains 75 items, including five non-scored questions, and allows 150 minutes for completion. The Calculations Portion contains 33 items, including three non-scored questions, and allows 170 minutes.

The NEC Knowledge Portion covers definitions, electrical theory, plan reading, electrical services, service equipment, separately derived systems, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, electrical wiring methods and materials, equipment and devices, motors, generators, control devices, disconnecting means, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, photovoltaic systems, and electric vehicle chargers.

Services and service equipment are a major examination area. Candidates should understand load requirements, fault-current concepts, service equipment, temporary wiring, transformers, grounding, bonding, and separately derived systems.

Branch-circuit preparation should include conductor requirements, GFCI and AFCI protection, overcurrent protection, grounding and bonding conductors, conductor ampacity, and applicable installation requirements.

Wiring methods and materials include raceways, cable trays, boxes, enclosures, cabinets, panels, cables, cords, and underground wiring. Candidates should understand permitted applications, installation limitations, support requirements, fill calculations, and physical protection.

Equipment and device questions can involve receptacles, switches, luminaires, air-conditioning equipment, refrigeration equipment, heating equipment, appliances, and workspace clearance. Motors and generators represent another important technical area requiring both code knowledge and calculation skills.

The Calculations Portion focuses on electrical theory and mathematical applications involving services, separately derived systems, feeders, branch circuits, conductors, grounding and bonding, raceways, equipment, motors, generators, controls, special installations, and renewable-energy technologies.

Simulated practice exams included with this package help students work through technical questions in an exam-oriented format. Reviewing missed questions helps identify whether additional study is needed in calculations, code navigation, theory, services, branch circuits, motors, or other subjects.

Open Book Test

The Texas Master Electrician examination is an open-book test. Candidates may bring the approved National Electrical Code book into the examination room under the rules established by TDLR and PSI.

Through August 31, 2026, Texas electrician examinations are referenced to the 2023 National Electrical Code. Beginning September 1, 2026, the examinations will be referenced to the 2026 NEC. Candidates should use the code edition corresponding to their actual examination date.

For examinations currently based on the 2023 NEC, candidates are responsible for bringing their own acceptable copy of the National Electrical Code. Soft-bound editions are permitted. Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, and ring-bound copies are not permitted.

The NEC may be highlighted, underlined, and contain notes prepared before entering the examination session. Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, index, or otherwise mark the NEC during the examination.

Permanent index tabs manufactured or provided for the NEC book are permitted. Homemade, do-it-yourself, or non-NEC tabs are not permitted under the current examination rules. Candidates using the included tabs should prepare their exam-room code book only in a manner that complies with the current PSI bulletin.

Additional paper, whether loose, taped, adhered, or stapled to the book, is prohibited. The NEC Handbook is also not permitted in the examination room.

An open-book examination still requires strong preparation. Candidates do not have unlimited time to search for every rule. The NEC Knowledge Portion allows 150 minutes for 75 items, while the Calculations Portion allows 170 minutes for 33 items.

Students should practice recognizing the subject of each question first and then moving directly to the applicable article, table, definition, or exception. Regular code-book navigation during the course and simulated exams can make reference research faster and more consistent.

Licensing Steps

Begin by meeting the experience qualifications established by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. To apply for a Texas Master Electrician license through the standard experience-and-examination pathway, an applicant must have held a Texas Journeyman Electrician license for at least two years.

The applicant must also document at least 12,000 hours of on-the-job electrical training performed under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician. Journeyman Industrial Electrician experience does not qualify toward the Master Electrician license under the standard pathway.

Obtain experience verification from the Master Electricians who supervised the qualifying work. TDLR requires the applicable experience documentation as part of the Master Electrician application process.

Submit the Master Electrician license application to TDLR. After the application and supporting documentation have been reviewed and approved, TDLR provides authorization to take the licensing examination.

Schedule the examination through PSI after receiving eligibility. Applicants have one year from the date TDLR receives the application to satisfy the licensing requirements, including passing the examination.

Create a preparation schedule that addresses both examination portions. Begin with the NEC overview and organize the 2023 NEC for efficient study navigation. Work through the Master Electrician Study Guide by examination subject and use the Calculations Study Guide for focused mathematical practice.

Use flash cards regularly to reinforce formulas, definitions, terminology, and electrical concepts. Participate in live instructor-led sessions and use recorded videos to revisit challenging material.

Complete simulated practice exams under realistic time limits. Practice the NEC Knowledge Portion separately from calculation-intensive testing so you can develop appropriate pacing for each part.

After passing both examination portions, complete the remaining TDLR licensing requirements. Once the Master Electrician license is issued, maintain the credential through annual renewal and continuing education.

State Requirements

The Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation administers statewide electrician licensing. Except for specific exemptions established by law, individuals performing electrical work in Texas must hold the appropriate electrical license.

A Texas Master Electrician license authorizes advanced electrical work involving electrical wiring systems and associated apparatus and equipment. Master Electricians may also play an important role in electrical contracting businesses.

To qualify through the standard experience pathway, an applicant must have held a Texas Journeyman Electrician license for at least two years and document 12,000 hours of on-the-job electrical training under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.

Texas also requires an Electrical Contractor business to either be owned by a properly licensed Master Electrician who assigns the license to the company or employ a licensed Master Electrician who serves as the Master Electrician of Record.

Texas adopted the 2023 National Electrical Code as the minimum electrical standard for covered electrical work beginning September 1, 2023. Municipalities may establish local amendments within the authority provided by Texas law.

Texas Master Electrician licenses are valid for one year and must be renewed annually. Master Electricians must complete four hours of approved continuing education during each license cycle before renewal.

The state’s examination code edition is scheduled to change from the 2023 NEC to the 2026 NEC on September 1, 2026. Candidates should distinguish between the code edition used in this 2023 study package and the edition that applies to the examination date they select.

Licensed electricians and electrical contractors must also comply with applicable permitting, inspection, local amendment, supervision, and electrical contractor requirements. Holding the Master Electrician license does not eliminate project-specific permit or inspection obligations.

Test Information and Study Materials

An effective Texas Master Electrician study plan should prepare separately for NEC Knowledge and Calculations while maintaining regular practice in both areas. The two-part testing structure makes it important to develop both code-research efficiency and mathematical accuracy.

Begin with the NEC overview to understand how the National Electrical Code is organized. Practice using the table of contents, index, definitions, chapters, articles, tables, exceptions, and cross-references.

Use the Master Electrician Study Guide to organize preparation around the major examination subjects. Focus on services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, equipment, motors, generators, special occupancies, and renewable-energy systems.

Use the Calculations Study Guide to strengthen service loads, feeder calculations, branch circuits, conductor sizing, transformers, grounding, motors, generators, raceway calculations, and related mathematical topics.

Practice writing out complete calculations rather than relying on mental shortcuts. Identify the known values, select the applicable formula, locate any required NEC table or rule, perform the calculation, and check the result.

Flash cards provide active-recall practice for definitions, formulas, and terminology. Live instructor-led NEC lessons add organized guidance, while recorded videos allow candidates to replay difficult examples and review material around their schedules.

Simulated practice exams should be completed under timed conditions. Use approximately the same pacing required by the official NEC Knowledge and Calculations portions so that time management becomes part of the preparation process.

After every simulated exam, review incorrect answers and slow questions. Identify whether each problem resulted from weak theory knowledge, a calculation error, unfamiliar terminology, inefficient code navigation, or overlooking an exception or table note.
