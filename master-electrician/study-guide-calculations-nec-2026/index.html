Licensing Steps

Texas Master Electrician licensing is handled through TDLR. A typical path includes holding a qualifying journeyman electrician license, documenting the required electrical work experience, completing the TDLR master electrician application, receiving eligibility approval, scheduling the exam through PSI, preparing with code-based study materials, passing the required examination, and completing final licensing steps with TDLR.

Texas Master Electrician applicants must meet the state’s experience requirements before approval. The master license path requires substantial on-the-job training under the supervision of a master electrician and a qualifying period as a licensed journeyman electrician. Candidates should organize their employment history, supervision information, license history, and work-hour documentation before applying.

The Texas master electrician license path is not the same as the journeyman electrician path. A journeyman license confirms a lower-level individual electrical credential, while a master electrician license is an advanced credential for electricians who have progressed through additional experience and examination requirements. Candidates should make sure their application matches the license level they are pursuing.

After TDLR approves examination eligibility, candidates follow PSI instructions to schedule the exam. Before test day, candidates should review all examination rules, approved references, calculator requirements, identification requirements, arrival instructions, prohibited items, score reporting, and retesting procedures. The candidate information bulletin should be reviewed before relying on any exam-day assumption.

Texas licensees are subject to continuing education requirements for renewal. How does the Master Electrician exam work?

The Master Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

  • The PSI exam has two portions: 75 NEC knowledge items in 150 minutes and 33 calculation items in 170 minutes.
  • The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $45.
  • TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
  • Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
  • The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
  • Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
