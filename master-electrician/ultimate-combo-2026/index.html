Exam Details

The Texas Master Electrician examination is part of the state licensing process administered through TDLR, with examination delivery handled through PSI. Candidates must apply through TDLR, receive eligibility approval, and then follow PSI instructions to schedule the exam. The examination is designed to measure whether the candidate has the knowledge and judgment expected of an experienced master electrician working under Texas electrical licensing rules.

To qualify for a Texas Master Electrician license, an applicant must have at least 12,000 hours of on-the-job training under the supervision of a master electrician and must have held a journeyman electrician license for at least two years before taking the master electrician exam. Candidates should make sure their work history and license history are documented accurately before applying.

The Texas Master Electrician exam is an advanced electrical licensing exam. State Requirements

Texas uses a state-level electrical licensing structure through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. TDLR regulates electricians and electrical contractors under Texas electrical laws and administrative rules. Candidates preparing for a Texas Master Electrician license should follow the state licensing process rather than a local-only license path.

The Texas Master Electrician license is an advanced individual electrical license. To qualify, applicants must have the required supervised on-the-job training and must have held the required journeyman electrician license before taking the master exam. Journeyman lineman and journeyman industrial electrician licenses are limited licenses and are not the same as the journeyman electrician license for Texas master electrician qualification purposes.

Texas also separates individual licensing from contractor business licensing. A master electrician may be able to serve as the master of record for an electrical contractor, but an individual license alone is not the same as an electrical contractor business license. How does the Master Electrician exam work?

The Master Electrician exam is a TDLR license exam given by PSI. TDLR approves your application first, then you schedule with PSI and have one year from the application date to pass.

  • The PSI exam has two portions: 75 NEC knowledge items in 150 minutes and 33 calculation items in 170 minutes.
  • The exam fee is $78 for both portions and the TDLR application fee is $45.
  • TDLR licenses Texas electricians, and PSI gives every exam with a 70 percent passing score on each portion.
  • Since September 1, 2026, the exams are written to the 2026 National Electrical Code, the edition Texas adopted on July 28, 2026.
  • The exams are open book: bring a soft-bound NEC, highlighted and noted before the exam, with only the publisher's permanent index tabs. Homemade and other tabs are not allowed.
  • Licenses renew every year with 4 hours of continuing education covering the NEC, Texas electrician law and rules, and NFPA 70E.
