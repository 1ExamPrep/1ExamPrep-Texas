### Open Book Test

The Texas Journeyman Sign Electrician examination is an **open book test**.

The approved technical reference is the **National Electrical Code, NEC, 2026 Edition**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own acceptable NEC book to the testing center.

Any acceptable soft-bound edition of the 2026 NEC may be used. Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, and ring-bound NEC copies are not permitted in the examination room. Candidates should make sure the physical copy they plan to bring complies with the examination rules before test day.

Because the examination is open book, efficient NEC navigation should be part of regular preparation. Candidates should practice locating sign requirements, definitions, branch-circuit rules, service provisions, conductor tables, grounding and bonding requirements, and wiring methods without spending excessive time searching.

Article 600 should become especially familiar. Candidates should know how sign requirements interact with other NEC chapters rather than treating Article 600 as an isolated section. A sign question may require information from Article 600 together with requirements for conductors, grounding, branch circuits, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, disconnects, or transformers.

The NEC can help confirm a code rule, but it does not replace electrical understanding. Candidates still need to recognize which requirement applies and how to use the information found in the code to answer the question.

### Test Information and Study Materials

**Electric Signs and Outline Lighting:** This is the most important individual category on the examination, accounting for 31 scored items. Study Article 600 thoroughly, including general requirements for electric signs and outline lighting, installation, listing and marking, branch circuits, disconnects, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, and equipment.

**Sign Disconnecting Means:** Review the location, accessibility, identification, and operating requirements that apply to disconnecting means for signs and outline-lighting systems. Understand when disconnects are required and how they relate to sign equipment.

**Branch Circuits for Signs:** Study branch-circuit sizing, conductor requirements, overcurrent protection, and the NEC provisions specifically affecting signs. Candidates should understand both Article 600 rules and the general branch-circuit requirements in Article 210.

**Definitions, Calculations, and Theory:** Ten scored items cover basic electrical theory, calculations, terminology, and NEC definitions. Review voltage, current, resistance, power, load calculations, conductor relationships, and definitions that influence sign installations.

**Services and Service Equipment:** Eight questions involve services, service equipment, and separately derived systems. Study service conductors, service ratings, disconnecting means, service equipment, grounding, bonding, and installation requirements.

**Feeders:** Review feeder conductor sizing, overcurrent protection, grounding, neutral requirements, and how feeders serve sign circuits and related electrical equipment.

**Branch Circuit Calculations and Conductors:** Ten scored items cover branch circuits and conductors. Study conductor ampacity, continuous loads, overcurrent protection, required circuits, temperature limitations, and conductor sizing.

**Grounding and Bonding:** Sign installations require careful attention to grounding and bonding. Review Article 250 requirements, equipment grounding conductors, bonding of metal parts, grounding paths, and the relationship between sign equipment and the electrical system supplying it.

**Wiring Methods and Electrical Materials:** Six scored items address wiring methods. Review Chapter 3 raceways, cables, conductor installation, boxes, enclosures, support, physical protection, wet locations, and outdoor installations.

**Outdoor and Wet Locations:** Electrical signs are frequently installed outdoors. Review weatherproof and weather-resistant equipment requirements, wet-location wiring methods, raceway provisions, enclosures, fittings, conductor insulation, and protection from environmental exposure.

**Electrical Equipment and Devices:** Eight questions cover electrical equipment and devices. Study transformers, switches, luminaires, overcurrent devices, power supplies, controllers, and other equipment associated with electrical sign installations.

**Motors:** The examination includes a smaller motor category, but candidates should still know basic Article 430 requirements relating to motor conductors, protection, controls, and disconnecting means when motor-operated equipment is involved.

**Control Devices:** Review switches, relays, contactors, time controls, photocells, and other control equipment that may be used to operate signs or outline lighting.

**NEC Article 600 Navigation:** Practice finding Article 600 requirements quickly. Pay attention to cross-references that direct the user to other NEC articles because many exam questions require applying a general code requirement together with a sign-specific provision.

**Transformers and Power Supplies:** Signs and lighting systems may use transformers or power supplies. Review installation, overcurrent protection, grounding, conductor requirements, and applicable equipment provisions.

**LED Sign Systems:** Modern signs commonly use light-emitting-diode technologies. Candidates should understand how the NEC treats sign equipment, power supplies, wiring, disconnects, grounding, and installation requirements regardless of the specific lighting technology used.

**Outline Lighting:** Study the NEC requirements that apply specifically to outline-lighting systems, including wiring, equipment, disconnecting means, and installation.

**Raceways and Boxes:** Review raceway fill, box sizing, fittings, conductor protection, supports, and outdoor installation considerations. Sign work frequently involves transitions between building wiring and sign equipment.

**Conductor Ampacity:** Practice using the Article 310 ampacity tables and applying temperature limitations, adjustment factors, correction factors, and termination requirements.

**Overcurrent Protection:** Understand how branch-circuit and equipment overcurrent protection applies to sign loads. Review Article 240 and the related requirements referenced by Article 600.

**Timed Reference Practice:** Candidates have 240 minutes for 80 scored items. Practice solving both direct code questions and calculation questions under time limits so NEC searches do not consume too much of the testing period.
