How does the ICC Master Plumber with Gas exam work?

The ICC Master Plumber with Gas exam is a national exam. It is listed here for jurisdictions that use it; Texas state trade licenses have their own exams.

  • ICC National Standard and NASCLA exams are national exams, used by cities, counties and other states that do not write their own.
  • Texas licenses electricians and air conditioning contractors through TDLR and plumbers through TSBPE, so these exams are mainly for work outside the state system.
  • ICC exams are open book for most categories, and the ICC bulletin for the exam lists the references allowed.

Who issues the ICC Master Plumber with Gas license or certification in Texas?

It comes from the city, county or state that uses the exam. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Can I use an ICC or NASCLA exam for a Texas state license?

Not for the TDLR electrician or A/C licenses or the TSBPE plumbing licenses, which have their own exams. These national exams are for cities, counties and other states that accept them.

Which code edition is the exam on?

Each ICC and NASCLA bulletin names the edition. Check it before ordering, since jurisdictions adopt new codes on their own schedules.
