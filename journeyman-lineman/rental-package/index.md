### Open Book Test

The Texas Journeyman Lineman Electrician examination is an **open book test**.

### Test Information and Study Materials

**Definitions and Theory:** Six scored items cover electrical terminology and theory. Review voltage, current, resistance, power, circuits, grounding terminology, conductor terminology, equipment definitions, and other concepts needed to interpret NEC questions correctly.

**Electrical Services:** Eight scored items cover electrical services, service equipment, and separately derived systems. Review service conductors, equipment ratings, service disconnects, overcurrent protection, grounding, bonding, and associated NEC provisions.

**Separately Derived Systems:** Review transformers, system bonding jumpers, grounding electrode conductors, equipment grounding conductors, and the grounding and bonding requirements associated with separately derived systems.

**Electrical Feeders:** Four scored items cover feeders. Study feeder conductors, overcurrent protection, grounding, bonding, equipment grounding conductors, and general feeder requirements.

**Branch Circuits:** Review branch-circuit conductors, overcurrent protection, equipment supply, grounding, and applicable general branch-circuit requirements.

**Electrical Wiring Methods and Materials:** Study conductors, raceways, cable systems, boxes, fittings, enclosures, physical protection, and the Chapter 3 requirements that may apply to electrical installations within the license scope.

**Electrical Equipment and Devices:** This is the largest examination category with 10 scored items. Candidates should spend substantial study time on equipment installation requirements, ratings, clearances, grounding, overcurrent protection, disconnecting means, and other rules associated with electrical devices and equipment.

**Equipment Ratings:** Understand voltage, current, interrupting ratings, equipment suitability, and the importance of installing electrical equipment within its listed and labeled limitations.

**Working Space and Equipment Access:** Review Article 110 requirements involving working clearances, guarding, accessibility, dedicated equipment space, installation workmanship, and equipment identification.

**Grounding and Bonding:** Article 250 supports many examination subjects. Review equipment grounding conductors, grounding electrode conductors, bonding jumpers, grounding electrodes, service bonding, and separately derived systems.

**Motors:** Study motor conductor sizing, overload protection, branch-circuit short-circuit and ground-fault protection, controllers, and disconnecting means.

**Generators:** Review generator installations, conductors, transfer equipment, grounding and bonding, disconnects, and other NEC requirements relevant to generator systems.

**Electrical Controls:** Six scored items are shared between control devices and disconnecting means. Review switches, controllers, relays, control equipment, ratings, locations, and applicable installation requirements.

**Disconnecting Means:** Study disconnect ratings, locations, accessibility, grouping requirements, identification, and the relationship between disconnects and the equipment they serve.

**Overcurrent Protection:** Review the relationship between conductor ampacity, equipment ratings, and protective devices. Understand basic overcurrent-protection principles and how they connect to services, feeders, motors, and equipment.

**Code Navigation:** Use the highlighted and tabbed 2026 NEC during regular study sessions. Practice recognizing the main subject of a question before opening the book, then move directly to the relevant article or table.

**Cross-Reference Practice:** NEC questions sometimes require information from more than one article. Practice following cross-references so that related requirements can be located without losing unnecessary time.

**Article 110:** General Requirements for Electrical Installations is an important study area because it contains rules affecting equipment installation, working space, interrupting ratings, labeling, environmental suitability, and workmanship.

**Article 230:** Review service conductors, service equipment, disconnecting means, overcurrent protection, and service installation requirements because services represent a significant portion of the examination.

**Article 250:** Grounding and bonding concepts can appear across services, equipment, feeders, generators, and separately derived systems. Candidates should be comfortable navigating this article.

**Article 430:** Review motor requirements, including conductors, overload protection, controllers, short-circuit and ground-fault protection, and disconnecting means.

**Timed Practice:** Candidates receive 120 minutes for 40 scored items. This provides an average of approximately three minutes per scored question. Practice maintaining a steady pace while preserving extra time for questions requiring detailed code searches.

**Study by Exam Weight:** Give strong attention to Electrical Equipment and Devices, Services and Separately Derived Systems, Definitions and Theory, and Electrical Control Devices and Disconnecting Means. Together, these subjects make up most of the scored examination.
