### Is the Code Books, Courses and Practice Tests exam open book?

Texas electrical and A/C exams are open book, and plumbing exams are closed book. Code books here are for the open-book exams and for studying.

### Who issues the Code Books, Courses and Practice Tests license or certification in Texas?

It comes from the board for the license you are testing for. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Which edition of a code book should I buy?

The edition named in the candidate bulletin for your exam. For Texas electrical exams that is the 2026 NEC since September 1, 2026.

### Can I bring a tabbed NEC into a Texas electrical exam?

Only with the publisher's permanent index tabs. PSI does not allow homemade or other tabs, and nothing may be added or marked during the exam.

### Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice exams are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations that point to the reference section.
