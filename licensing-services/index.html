How does the License Application and Business Setup Services exam work?

The License Application and Business Setup Services help covers the paperwork side of a Texas license, which every new contracting business goes through whatever its trade.

  • A Texas LLC or corporation is formed with a certificate of formation at the Secretary of State, which costs $300.
  • An Electrical Contractor license costs $110 and needs a Master Electrician plus $300,000 per occurrence liability insurance.
  • An ACR contractor license costs $115, with insurance of $300,000 per occurrence for Class A and $100,000 for Class B.
  • Neither license has a business and law exam in Texas; the trade exam is the only test.

Is the License Application and Business Setup Services exam open book?

There is no exam for the paperwork itself. The trade exam that goes with the license has its own rule: open book for electrical and A/C, closed book for plumbing.

Who issues the License Application and Business Setup Services license or certification in Texas?

It comes from TDLR for the trade license and the Texas Secretary of State for the business entity. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

How much does it cost to form a Texas LLC?

The Texas Secretary of State charges $300 to file a certificate of formation for an LLC or a for-profit corporation. Our service fee is separate and shown at checkout.

What does a Texas electrical contractor license need?

A Master Electrician on staff, who can also be the owner, liability insurance of $300,000 per occurrence and $600,000 aggregate, workers' compensation or a filed election, and a $110 TDLR fee.
