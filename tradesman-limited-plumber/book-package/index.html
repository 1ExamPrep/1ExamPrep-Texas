About this Tradesman Plumber-Limited package

Prepare for the Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited examination with a focused collection of residential plumbing, fuel-gas, and plumbing-code references. This package includes the 2018 International Residential Code for One- and Two-Family Dwellings, 2018 International Plumbing Code, 2018 International Fuel Gas Code, and 2018 Uniform Plumbing Code to support organized study for the knowledge and practical skills involved in Texas residential plumbing work.

The Tradesman Plumber-Limited license is administered by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE). It is designed for plumbing professionals whose licensed scope centers on one- and two-family dwellings. A Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited may construct, install, repair, service, or renovate plumbing for one- and two-family dwellings while working under the supervision of a Responsible Master Plumber.

The licensing examination includes both a computer-based written component and, for applicants coming from the Plumber's Apprentice level, a practical hands-on component. The written examination is administered through Pearson VUE, while the practical portion is administered through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners at designated Texas examination locations.

Preparing for this license requires more than familiarity with pipe and fittings. Candidates should understand water distribution, drainage, waste and vent systems, plumbing fixtures, water heaters, fuel-gas installations, plumbing-code requirements, residential system layouts, and proper installation practices. The practical portion also requires candidates to demonstrate hands-on plumbing abilities rather than relying only on written knowledge.

The books in this package provide a strong technical foundation for those study areas. Candidates can use them to reinforce code terminology, residential plumbing principles, water and drainage requirements, gas-piping provisions, fixture installation, venting, materials, sizing, and other subjects connected with the Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited examination.

Exam Details

The Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited examination consists of a computer-based written examination followed, when applicable, by a practical examination component.

  • License: Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited
  • Licensing Agency: Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners
  • Written Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Written Examination Items: 85
  • Written Examination Time: 120 minutes
  • Practical Examination Items: 6
  • Practical Examination Time: 240 minutes
  • Exam Structure: Computer-based written examination plus practical testing when applicable

The written examination evaluates plumbing knowledge across subjects relevant to residential plumbing work. The practical portion requires applicants to demonstrate installation skills using materials furnished for the examination.

Applicants coming from the Plumber's Apprentice level generally proceed to the practical portion after successfully completing the computer-based examination. Pearson VUE communicates written examination results to TSBPE, and successful candidates receive instructions for scheduling the practical component.

The practical examination may be scheduled at designated TSBPE testing locations. Candidates should be prepared to demonstrate actual plumbing installation skills and comply with examination-site clothing and safety requirements.

Closed Book Test

The computer-based Texas plumbing examination is a closed book test. Personal books, dictionaries, papers, scratch paper, study guides, and other reference materials are not permitted in the Pearson VUE examination room.

Because candidates cannot rely on their plumbing books during the written examination, preparation should focus on understanding and retaining the rules rather than simply learning where information appears in a codebook. The code references in this package are study materials used before examination day.

Candidates should repeatedly review common residential plumbing requirements until they can recognize correct installation methods from memory. Study should include drainage slopes, venting principles, fixture requirements, water distribution, water heaters, gas piping, material limitations, cleanouts, traps, sizing, and residential plumbing layouts.

The closed-book format makes practice questions and active recall particularly useful. After reviewing a code topic, candidates should test themselves without looking at the book, then return to the reference to correct mistakes and reinforce the applicable rule.

The practical examination is also skills-based. TSBPE provides the materials needed for that portion, so candidates should arrive prepared to demonstrate installation abilities rather than depend on personal study references.

Licensing Steps

  1. Hold the appropriate Texas registration or qualifying out-of-state license. Applicants generally begin as a Texas Plumber's Apprentice, although qualifying Journeyman or Master Plumbers licensed in another state may also meet an eligibility pathway.
  2. Accumulate qualifying plumbing experience. The standard Tradesman Plumber-Limited pathway requires at least 4,000 hours of experience working in the plumbing trade.
  3. Complete the required examination training. Applicants generally must complete the TSBPE-approved 24-hour training program unless an applicable exemption applies.
  4. Complete fingerprint requirements. Texas requires plumbing examination applicants to have the required fingerprint information on file.
  5. Submit the Tradesman Plumber-Limited examination application. Include the required supporting documentation and employer experience verification.
  6. Receive Authorization to Test. After TSBPE approves the application, the candidate receives testing authorization.
  7. Schedule the computer-based examination. The written examination is scheduled through Pearson VUE.
  8. Pass the written examination. Successful candidates who require the practical component then receive additional scheduling instructions.
  9. Complete the practical examination when required. The practical portion evaluates hands-on plumbing installation abilities.
  10. Complete the license issuance process. After satisfying the examination requirements, complete the remaining TSBPE licensing steps for issuance of the Tradesman Plumber-Limited license.

State Requirements

The standard Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited examination pathway requires applicants to be registered as a Plumber's Apprentice in Texas and to have at least 4,000 hours of qualifying plumbing experience.

Applicants must also complete a 24-hour TSBPE-approved training program unless they qualify for an exemption. The training program includes continuing professional education, OSHA construction-industry safety training, and instruction involving the International Residential Code and/or Uniform Plumbing Code.

Applicants enrolled in or having completed an apprenticeship program approved by the United States Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship may qualify under an alternative training pathway. Certain holders of current out-of-state Journeyman or Master Plumber licenses may also be exempt from the 24-hour training requirement.

A Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited may construct, install, repair, service, or renovate plumbing for one- and two-family dwellings under the supervision of a Responsible Master Plumber.

A licensed Tradesman Plumber-Limited may directly supervise a Plumber's Apprentice on one- and two-family dwelling job sites. When performing plumbing work in a structure other than a one- or two-family dwelling, the Tradesman Plumber-Limited must work under the direct supervision of a licensed Journeyman or Master Plumber who is present at the job site.

Texas plumbing licenses renew annually. Licensees must complete the continuing professional education required by TSBPE to remain eligible for renewal.

  • International Plumbing Code, 2018

    Covers fuel-gas piping, appliance connections, shutoff valves, pressure testing, combustion air, venting, gas appliances, and related installation and safety requirements. This reference is particularly useful for reviewing gas-fired water heaters and residential fuel-gas systems.
  • Uniform Plumbing Code, 2018
    A plumbing-code reference addressing plumbing fixtures, potable water systems, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, water heaters, fuel-gas systems, materials, sizing, and installation practices used in plumbing exam preparation.

Test Information and Study Materials

Water Supply and Distribution: Review residential water-service piping, distribution piping, fixture supply requirements, sizing, pressure, valves, materials, installation, testing, and protection against contamination.

Sanitary Drainage: Study drainage-system layout, drainage fixture units, pipe sizing, slope, fittings, changes in direction, building drains, building sewers, cleanouts, and proper installation methods.

Venting Systems: Understand the purpose of plumbing vents and the methods used to protect trap seals. Review vent sizing, vent connections, individual vents, common vents, wet venting, and termination requirements.

Traps: Review trap requirements, trap seals, prohibited traps, trap-arm limitations, fixture connections, and the relationship between traps and venting systems.

Plumbing Fixtures: Study installation requirements for water closets, lavatories, sinks, bathtubs, showers, laundry fixtures, and other residential plumbing fixtures.

Water Heaters: Water-heater installation is an important study area. Review approved locations, supply valves, temperature and pressure relief valves, discharge piping, drain pans, combustion air, gas shutoff valves, and venting requirements.

Fuel Gas: Use the 2018 International Fuel Gas Code to review gas-pipe sizing, appliance connections, shutoff valves, pressure testing, regulators, sediment traps, combustion air, venting, and safe installation of gas-fired appliances.

Materials: Become familiar with commonly permitted piping materials and their approved applications. Review joining methods, fittings, supports, corrosion protection, and installation requirements.

Plumbing Calculations: Practice code-based sizing involving drainage fixture units, water-supply fixture units, pipe dimensions, developed lengths, and other calculations relevant to residential plumbing.

Residential Plumbing Layout: Review how water, drainage, waste, vent, and gas systems fit together within one- and two-family dwellings. Understanding complete system layouts can help candidates identify installation problems more easily.

Code Terminology: Learn the definitions used by the plumbing codes. Correct interpretation of terms such as building drain, building sewer, fixture drain, vent, trap arm, developed length, and water service is essential when answering technical questions.

Practical Skills: Written knowledge should be reinforced with hands-on practice. Candidates should be comfortable with measuring, cutting, assembling, supporting, and connecting plumbing materials according to accepted installation procedures.

Installation Errors: Practice identifying common code violations involving water heaters, drainage, venting, traps, piping support, connections, valves, and gas appliances. The ability to recognize incorrect installations supports both written and practical preparation.

Closed-Book Recall: Because books and study guides are not permitted in the computer-based examination room, candidates should periodically study without references. Review a topic, close the book, and explain the rule or solve practice questions from memory.
